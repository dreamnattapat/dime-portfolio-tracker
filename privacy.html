Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Dime! Portfolio Tracker ("the app") is a free, open-source tool that builds a transaction history from the trade confirmation emails the Dime! investment app sends to your Gmail. It is run by an individual developer and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Dime! or KKP Dime Securities Company Limited.

In short: your data is processed only in your own web browser. The app has no server that receives, stores, or can read your emails, your birthdate, or your trades.

What the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only). With your permission, the app uses Google's gmail.readonly access to search your mailbox for Dime! trade confirmation emails (by their subject line) and download their PDF attachments. It does not request any other emails, and it cannot send, delete, or change anything in your mailbox.
  • Your birthdate. Dime! protects its PDFs with your birthdate. You type it into the page so the app can open the PDFs. It is kept only in the page's memory, is never saved or sent anywhere, and is forgotten when you reload or close the page.
  • Market prices. To value your holdings and compare them with the S&P 500, the page looks up daily prices for the ticker symbols you've traded (plus SPY and the USD/THB rate). These lookups go to this site's own price service, which fetches them from Yahoo Finance. Only a ticker symbol and a date range are sent: no amounts, units, account details or anything else about your trades. The price service doesn't log or store the lookups.

Where your data goes

  • Emails and PDFs are downloaded directly from Google's servers into your browser. They are opened and read there, on your device.
  • The transactions found are saved in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on that device only. They are not uploaded anywhere and are not visible to the developer.
  • The site's security settings (its Content Security Policy) only allow the page to connect to itself and to Google, so the browser itself blocks any attempt to send your data to another server.
  • The Google access token is kept in the page's memory for up to about an hour, used only to talk to Google, and never stored.

What the developer can see

Nothing from your Gmail, your birthdate, or your trades. The site is hosted on Cloudflare: static files, plus the small price service described above, which only ever sees ticker symbols and dates. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes basic technical request information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages and protect against abuse, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The app does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Google sign-in

Signing in happens on Google's own pages; the app never sees your Google password. Google's sign-in service is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Dime! Portfolio Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from your Gmail is used only to show you your own transaction history, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by any person.

Sharing and selling

The app does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone. It has no access to your data to share.

Removing your data and access

  • Stored transactions: click Delete local data at the bottom of the app's page, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Gmail access: click Disconnect in the app, or remove the app at any time from your Google account's third-party connections.

Verify it yourself

The full source code is public at github.com/dreamnattapat/dime-portfolio-tracker. You can also watch your browser's developer tools (Network tab) while using the app: requests go only to the site itself and to Google.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, please open an issue on GitHub.
